Put MIDI on YOUR homepage!!

Just paste this into your HTML. It works with your old tags too:

<SCRIPT SRC="crescendo/crescendo.js"></SCRIPT>

<EMBED SRC="mysong.mid" AUTOSTART=TRUE LOOP=TRUE WIDTH=200 HEIGHT=55>
<BGSOUND SRC="mysong.mid" LOOP=INFINITE>
<A HREF="mysong.mid">Click here for music!</A>

Want the fancy wavetable sound? Add data-soundfont="mybank.sf2" to the SCRIPT tag.
